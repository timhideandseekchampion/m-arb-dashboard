Trading fees

Every position in this ledger is charged at moomoo's published retail brokerage rates, per moomoo's fee schedule, including exchange, regulatory and government charges where they apply. One cost is not captured: financing and borrow, which the broker prices per security rather than publishing as a flat rate.

Slippage on entry

Every entry is charged a measured slippage allowance on top of fees, set at the pessimistic end of the achievable fill range rather than the best case. Size is not modelled, so a position large enough to move its own fill would cost more.
