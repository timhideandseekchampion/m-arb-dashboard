Update

Two risk overlays are now live in this ledger, both validated walk-forward after digging into where the largest historical drawdowns actually came from: a two-sided liquidity filter (dropping legs too thin to fill at this book's size and legs too liquid to carry real edge) and a smooth VIX-based de-risking of exposure. See the backtest page for the full methodology and a capacity disclosure — this is a concentrated, small-cap book with a real ceiling on how much it can absorb. A separate intraday entry-timing refinement was investigated too but isn't used: what looked like a clean improvement on a straight in-sample/out-of-sample split turned out, on closer inspection, to be an average of a genuinely good sub-population and a significantly worse one, with no real-time-available way found yet to tell them apart.

Round-trip, at a representative $7,500 per-pair position — moomoo's published retail fee schedule, HK stocks and US stocks.

LegBroker (moomoo)Exchange / regulatory Government taxTotal
HK (buy + sell)11.1bps2.4bps20.0bps33.5bps

HK broker fee: 0.03% commission (min HK$3) + HK$15/order platform fee. Exchange/regulatory: trading tariff, settlement fee, HKEX trading fee, SFC/FRC levy. Government: HK stamp duty, 0.1% each way. US stocks carry $0 commission and $0 platform fee on moomoo's resident schedule; the SEC fee and TAF apply only on the sell/cover side and are near-zero. Not included: the stock-borrow cost for shorting the ADR leg, which moomoo prices per-security and isn't published as a flat rate — a real cost this doesn't capture.
