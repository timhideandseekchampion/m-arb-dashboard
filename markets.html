Cost sensitivity · market expansion

What does this cost, and does it change by market?

The HK strategy's edge is measured net of a real, quoted spread. Extending the same construction to Korea, Japan and Taiwan, no equivalent measured spread exists there — so this prices all four markets at three cost tiers side by side, from a conservative retail assumption up to what the numbers would look like with institutional-grade execution.

This is original research, not a published strategy. The instrument universe, signal thresholds, and cost model are my own and aren't disclosed here — this page shows the cost methodology and its sensitivity only. Built and tested independently — happy to discuss the methodology in more depth: timliang228@gmail.com.

Three tiers, one question each

retailFull spread-crossing cost, standard retail commission — the original, conservative assumption.

realisticSpread corrected by the measured/estimated ratio from Hong Kong, same commission — the best estimate this data actually supports for Korea, Japan and Taiwan.

professionalRealistic spread with an added execution-quality discount (limit orders and algos, not retail market orders) and institutional commission — what this would look like with that access, not a claim that it exists today.

Statutory tax and stamp duty are held fixed across every tier, in every market: Hong Kong's, Taiwan's and Korea's are law, not a fee schedule, and no trader status changes them without specific market-maker registration, which isn't assumed here.
