Backtest

A systematic market-neutral arbitrage strategy that trades a short-lived, recurring dislocation in a liquid international equity universe. Backtested over its full available history, net of assumed costs (fees plus each name's measured exit spread) with execution modelled on measured fills, and two risk overlays described below.

This is original research, not a published strategy. This page shows performance only. The instrument universe, signal thresholds, cost model, and detailed trade logs are my own and aren't disclosed here. Built and backtested independently. Capacity

A small-cap book by design. It only trades when a name actually dislocates, so some days it holds a handful of positions and some days none at all. This puts a limit on how much capital can be implemented.

No position may exceed a fixed share of the volume it can be filled against, forecast from the previous ten sessions, since the size is fixed before that volume trades. What the open cannot fill is bought back at the next US open; the figures above include it.

The ratios therefore fall as size rises. They are computed on the $100,000 the dollar equity curve is drawn at.
