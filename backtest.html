Backtest

A systematic market-neutral arbitrage strategy that trades a short-lived, recurring dislocation in a liquid international equity universe. Backtested over its full available history, net of assumed costs, with execution modelled on measured fills.

This is original research, not a published strategy. This page shows performance only. The instrument universe, signal thresholds, cost model, and detailed trade logs are my own and aren't disclosed here. Built and backtested independently. If you'd like to discuss the methodology in more depth, reach out: timliang228@gmail.com.

Every figure is a flat $250,000 book re-traded each day: profits are not reinvested, so returns are simple, not compounded. Small capacity by design: on a $750k book Sharpe falls to 2.8 (20%/yr). See Capacity below.

Capacity

A small-capacity book by design. It only trades when a name actually dislocates, so some days it holds a handful of positions and some days none at all.

No position may exceed a fixed share of the volume it can be filled against, so the ratios fall as size rises. They are computed on the $250,000 the equity curve is drawn at; on a $750,000 book the Sharpe ratio is about 2.9.
