Beta and correlation

Beta against six benchmarks spanning both trading sessions, with 95% confidence intervals, shown against the exposure a fully invested book would carry. Largest beta 0.044, largest correlation 0.039, and no benchmark explains more than 0.15% of the variance.

Capacity

A small-cap book by design. It only trades when a name actually dislocates, so some days it holds a handful of positions and some days none at all. This puts a limit on how much capital can be implemented.

No position is allowed to exceed 5% of the traded value of the leg it has to be filled in, measured on the day it trades. Roughly an eighth of positions are held below their intended size by that rule, and the figures above are quoted after it.

The ratios therefore fall as size rises. They are computed on the $100,000 the dollar equity curve is drawn at.
